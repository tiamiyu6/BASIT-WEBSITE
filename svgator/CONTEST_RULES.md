## The brief
**Build an animation template someone else can make their own.**
Make one animation good enough that anyone could drop their own brand into it in five minutes and ship it.

Extra: the best entries end up in the SVGator template gallery, credited to you with a link to your portfolio.

## What you're making
One SVGator project, with a video and a short description. Three hard requirements:
1. **Editable text:** real text someone can retype, not outlined.
2. **Named elements:** "logo", "headline", "product-image". Not "Group 47".
3. **Replaceable images:** each photo or logo in its own element, sized so a different one drops in easily.

Pick a category people need: ads, UI elements, loaders, logos, microinteractions, animated assets. Check the gallery and make what's missing.

## How to build it (any of these)
1. **Bring your own artwork.** Paste from Illustrator or Figma, or drag the SVG in, then animate it. *(This is what we did.)*
2. **Start from another template.** Open one, take it apart, build your own.
3. **Use the SVGator MCP.** Generate and edit from an AI assistant, finish by hand.

## Submission requirements (every submission must include all four)
1. **The template:** your SVGator project, shared as a public link judges can open.
2. **The proof:** a video of it running, then the same template with a second set of content dropped in.
3. **The story:** two or three sentences on what it's best used for.
4. **The post:** a public post on X, Instagram or LinkedIn tagging **@SVGatorApp**, linked in your submission. It can be the same video and story you submit.

## Rules
- Past work counts.
- By submitting, you accept that your project may become a free-for-commercial-use template in the SVGator gallery, credited to you with a link to your portfolio.
- The artwork has to be yours to give away: no licensed stock, no borrowed icon sets, no fonts you can't redistribute.
- Multiple entries are welcome (submit them as separate posts).
