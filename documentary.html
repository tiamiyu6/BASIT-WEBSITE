    ABOUT THIS FILM

    From a Lagos childhood and Chicago accounting degree to the pro-democracy struggle, eight years as Governor of Lagos, the making of the APC and the 2023 presidency: this documentary traces the rise of Bola Ahmed Tinubu and the questions that have followed him, including the 1993 U.S. forfeiture case, the certificate dispute, #EndSARS, his opposition to fuel subsidy removal in 2012 and his own removal of it in 2023.

    Allegations are reported as allegations. Tinubu has denied wrongdoing in each case, and none of the controversies described has led to a criminal conviction. Illustrations are artistic reconstructions, not photographs. Narration is an AI voice. Sources: INEC, NBS, CBN, the Supreme Court of Nigeria judgment (October 2023), the Lagos State #EndSARS Judicial Panel report (2021) and contemporaneous news reporting.
