# JUNE 12: THE ELECTION NIGERIA STOLE
### Naija Untold, Documentary #2 · target length about 21 minutes

**Look:** Same quality as JAGABAN, but warmer and darker: 1990s newsprint grain, VHS archive texture on the footage, a gold-and-green palette, and clean modern titles.
**New animations:** an animated Naija Untold logo sting, a **live vote counter**, an **animated 1993 → 1998 timeline**, newspaper front pages that fly in and zoom, and a "two parties built by soldiers" diagram.
**Music:** one score that follows the story (details under each chapter). Talking drum and highlife guitar for MKO's rise, a low pulse for the plot, strings and choir for the losses, sunrise brass for the ending.
**Sound effects:** used only where they mean something. No random whooshes.

### FACT NOTES (handled carefully on screen)
- The 1993 vote totals are the **unofficial but widely reported** figures (NEC never completed the official declaration). On screen: *"unofficial results"*.
- Protest death toll: attributed to Human Rights Watch ("more than a hundred"); the US State Department said up to 150.
- Babangida's 2025 admission: quoted from the memoir launch (20 Feb 2025), including his claim that the annulment was announced without his permission.
- Abiola's death: the autopsy finding (heart disease) is stated, along with the family's doubts. We present the doubt as doubt, not as fact.
- Kudirat's killing: we state the confession and the court outcomes exactly, and name no other suspect.
