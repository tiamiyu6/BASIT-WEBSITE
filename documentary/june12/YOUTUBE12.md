## Description
On 12 June 1993, fourteen million Nigerians, Muslims and Christians, North and South, chose the same man: Chief Moshood Kashimawo Olawale Abiola. Eleven days later, the military cancelled it all.

This documentary tells the full story: the boy from Abeokuta whose name means "let us wait and see", the two parties built by soldiers, the cleanest election in Nigeria's history, the annulment, the Epetedo declaration, NADECO, the assassination of Kudirat Abiola, the cup of tea in July 1998, and Babangida's admission, thirty-two years later, that Abiola won.

Real archive footage, real newspapers, real voices.
