# WHY YOU'RE ALWAYS BROKE (EVEN WHEN YOU EARN): full script (draft 1)
Format: animated money-psychology explainer, same paper-cut style as the Japa film. About 6 minutes.
**For education only. Not financial advice.** Say this in the description, not in the video.
---

## COLD OPEN
1. **Your salary just landed. By the end of the week, it's gone.**
2. **You're not lazy. You're not stupid. And you're not alone.**
3. **Most people who feel broke don't have an earning problem.**
4. **They have a pattern problem.**
5. **And patterns can be changed.**
6. **In the next few minutes, I'll show you four hidden traps that keep money from staying in your hands.**
7. **And one simple system that works, even on a small income.**
8. **Let's start with the biggest trap of all.**

## CHAPTER 1: THE RAISE THAT DISAPPEARS
9. **Imagine you get a raise.**
10. **For a month, you feel rich.**
11. **Then the rent goes up. The food gets better. The phone gets upgraded.**
12. **A year later, you earn more, and you feel exactly as broke as before.**
13. **Economists call it lifestyle inflation.**
14. **Psychologists call the engine behind it hedonic adaptation: we get used to any improvement, very fast.**
15. **The new normal becomes the baseline.**
16. **So money that was meant to build your future quietly turns into bigger comfort today.**
17. **The fix isn't to suffer. It's to decide, before the raise arrives, what part of it belongs to your future.**

## CHAPTER 2: THE FAMILY FACTOR
18. **In many Nigerian homes, a good salary comes with a second job: being the family's safety net.**
19. **Some people call it black tax.**
20. **School fees. Hospital bills. Rent for a relative. A wedding contribution.**
21. **The love behind it is real.**
22. **But love without a plan can drain you.**
23. **If you have nothing left, you can't help anyone for long.**
24. **It's the same logic as the oxygen mask on a plane: secure yours first, so you can help others.**
25. **Decide a fixed amount for family each month. Tell them early. Give from the plan, not from panic.**
26. **A clear "yes, up to this much" protects both the money and the relationship.**

## CHAPTER 3: THE PRESSURE TO LOOK RICH
27. **Now think about owambe season.**
28. **Aso ebi. The gift. The new outfit. The photos.**
29. **In 1899, an economist called Thorstein Veblen noticed something: people often spend to show status, not just to meet needs.**
30. **He called it conspicuous consumption.**
31. **Today it plays out on your phone.**
32. **Everyone's life looks expensive online.**
33. **You don't see the debt behind the picture.**
34. **Spending to look rich is one of the fastest ways to stay poor.**
35. **Real wealth is quiet. It's the money you don't see, because it's working in the background.**

## CHAPTER 4: THE NAIRA PROBLEM
36. **Here's a trap that has nothing to do with your habits.**
37. **Prices keep rising.**
38. **When prices rise, the same cash buys less.**
39. **So money sitting in a drawer slowly loses its power.**
40. **Economists call this inflation.**
41. **That's why people say saving alone is not enough.**
42. **Money you won't need soon needs somewhere to grow, at a pace that at least keeps up with prices.**
43. **There are legal, regulated options: savings and fixed deposits, treasury bills, and mutual funds, among others.**
44. **Each one has different risks and returns, so learn how they work before you put money in.**
45. **And be careful with anything that promises quick, guaranteed, huge returns. If it sounds too good to be true, it usually is.**

## CHAPTER 5: THE SYSTEM
46. **So what actually works?**
47. **Not willpower. Willpower runs out.**
48. **What works is a system that moves your money before you can spend it.**
49. **Step one: pay yourself first. When salary lands, a fixed part goes to savings automatically. Even ten percent.**
50. **Step two: build an emergency fund, enough for a few months of basic costs.**
51. **Step three: give every remaining naira a job. Needs, wants, and future.**
52. **A popular rule of thumb splits it fifty, thirty, twenty. Adjust it to your life.**
53. **Step four: once your emergency fund is ready, start putting money into things that grow.**
54. **Automate all of it, so it happens even on days you forget.**
55. **Small, boring and automatic beats big, exciting and occasional.**

## ENDING
56. **Nobody gets rich in one month.**
57. **But anyone can change the pattern in one month.**
58. **This week, do one thing: set up an automatic transfer for salary day.**
59. **Even a small amount. Even five thousand naira.**
60. **Because wealth isn't about how much comes in.**
61. **It's about how much stays.**

**END CARD:** *What is the one money habit you are fixing this month? Tell us in the comments.*
