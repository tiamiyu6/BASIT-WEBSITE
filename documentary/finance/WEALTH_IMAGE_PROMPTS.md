# MONEY VIDEO: picture prompts (31 pictures)

Same method that worked for Japa. Make them in ChatGPT, inside a **Project** with the instructions below, one scene per message.

## Project instructions (paste once into a new ChatGPT Project called `Wealth`)

```
Flat 2D hand-cut paper collage illustration, visible paper texture, thick simple shapes, no outlines, soft film grain, warm earthy colours with deep green, gold, cream and terracotta. NOT a photograph, NOT 3D, NOT realistic, NOT cinematic. Wide 16:9, no text, no letters, no numbers. Kemi is a Nigerian woman in her late 20s, brown skin, braids tied in a bun, small gold earrings, teal blouse, and she looks the same in every picture. When I send "Picture NN: scene", draw exactly that scene.
```
