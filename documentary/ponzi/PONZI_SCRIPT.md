# WHY SMART NIGERIANS KEEP FALLING FOR PONZI SCHEMES: full script (draft 1)

Format: animated story, paper-cut style, about 5.5 minutes.

**Education only. Names only historical cases that are public record. Never accuse a current company by name.**

---

## COLD OPEN
1. **A message arrives on your phone. "Put in ten thousand naira. Get back thirteen thousand in thirty days."**
2. **It sounds too good to be true.**
3. **But then you notice something. Your friend got paid. Your cousin got paid. Even your neighbour got paid.**
4. **So you send the money.**
5. **This is how it starts. And this is how smart people lose everything.**
6. **This is the story of Ponzi schemes, and why Nigerians keep falling for them.**
7. **By the end, you will know how to spot one, before it spots you.**

## CHAPTER 1: THE ORIGINAL TRICK
8. **In 1920, a man named Charles Ponzi promised Americans a fifty percent return in forty-five days.**
9. **He said the profit came from buying and selling postal reply coupons across countries.**
10. **In truth, early investors were paid with the money of new investors.**
11. **That is the whole trick. No real business. Just new money paying old promises.**
12. **It only works while more and more people keep joining.**
13. **When new people stop coming, the whole thing falls. And the last people in lose everything.**
14. **More than a hundred years later, we still call it a Ponzi scheme.**

## CHAPTER 2: THE NIGERIAN CHAPTER
15. **In 2016, a platform called MMM arrived in Nigeria.**
16. **It was linked to a Russian man, Sergei Mavrodi, who had run schemes under the same name in other countries.**
17. **Members were told to "provide help" and later "get help", and the promise was thirty percent in a month.**
18. **A huge number of ordinary people joined, with savings, salaries and even loans.**
19. **For a while, the payouts came, and that was the hook.**
20. **Then, in December 2016, the platform froze withdrawals.**
21. **People who had put in their savings could not get their money back.**
22. **The scheme collapsed, as every one of them eventually does.**

## CHAPTER 3: WHY SMART PEOPLE FALL
23. **It is easy to say, "I would never fall for that."**
24. **But these schemes are not built for fools. They are built around how the human mind works.**
25. **First: social proof. When the people around us do something, we assume it must be safe.**
26. **Second: early wins. The first small payout feels like proof. Psychologists call this reinforcement.**
27. **Third: fear of missing out. Everyone is getting rich and you are watching.**
28. **Fourth: authority. A smiling man in a sharp suit, a big office, a famous name.**
29. **Fifth: the sunk cost trap. The more you put in, the harder it is to walk away.**
30. **And last: hope. When life is hard, hope is the easiest thing to sell.**
31. **That is why teachers, bankers and engineers have lost money too. It is not about intelligence. It is about pressure and hope.**

## CHAPTER 4: THE WARNING SIGNS
32. **So how do you spot one?**
33. **Sign one: guaranteed high returns. Real investments always carry risk. Nobody can promise you thirty percent a month.**
34. **Sign two: nobody can explain where the money comes from. If the answer is "trust us" or "it's complicated", walk away.**
35. **Sign three: pressure to bring in your friends. If your returns depend on recruiting more people, that is the shape of a pyramid.**
36. **Sign four: urgency. "Slots are closing tonight." Real investments do not vanish in an hour.**
37. **Sign five: no registration. Check whether the company is registered and licensed with the Securities and Exchange Commission, the SEC, before you send a single naira.**

## CHAPTER 5: PROTECT YOURSELF
38. **Before any investment, ask three questions.**
39. **One: who regulates it, and can I check that myself?**
40. **Two: how exactly does it make money, in plain words?**
41. **Three: if I lost all of this, would I be okay?**
42. **If the answer to any of these is no, do not put money in.**
43. **Never borrow to invest. Never use your rent or school fees. Never put in more than you can afford to lose.**
44. **If you have already lost money, you are not stupid, and you are not alone. Report it to the police or the SEC, and do not chase it by putting in more.**

## ENDING
45. **Real wealth is built slowly, openly, and from something you can explain.**
46. **It is boring on purpose.**
47. **If someone promises you fast money, ask yourself: who is paying for it?**
48. **Because in a Ponzi scheme, the answer is always the same. The next person.**
49. **Share this with someone who is thinking about "just trying small".**
