# MR MONEY: footage and photos to make it REAL

**Why we need your footage:** almost every photo of Asake online is owned by a photographer or a media house. If we use them, YouTube can strike the channel. Only **one** free-licensed photo of Asake exists (Toronto 2024), and the documentary already uses it.
The fix is what made Korty's video work: **real footage that is ours.** You can shoot all of this on your phone.

## How to shoot (applies to every clip)
- **Hold the phone sideways (landscape)**, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps.
- **10–20 seconds per clip.** Hold still, or move very slowly. Lean on a wall or table to steady it.
- Shoot early morning, golden hour (5–6:30 pm) or at night with some light. Avoid flat noon sun.
- **Don't film people's faces up close without asking.** Crowds, backs and hands are fine.
- **Never record Asake's songs playing on a speaker**: copyright music in the background gets flagged. Record silence or natural street sound only.
- Send the original files (Telegram as "File", or Google Drive), not WhatsApp, which compresses them.
