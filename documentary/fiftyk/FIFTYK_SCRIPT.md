# HOW TO START BUILDING WEALTH ON ₦50,000 A MONTH: full script (draft 1)

Format: animated story, paper-cut style, about 5 minutes.

**Education only. Not financial advice. No specific product or return is recommended or promised.**

---

## COLD OPEN
1. **Fifty thousand naira. That is Amaka's salary.**
2. **Rent takes a big part. Transport takes another. And by the 20th, her account is almost empty.**
3. **She thinks: how can I build wealth with this?**
4. **Here is the truth. You do not need a big salary to start. You need a small system.**
5. **In the next few minutes, I will show you five simple steps that work even on fifty thousand naira.**
6. **No tricks. No miracle investments. Just habits that grow quietly.**

## STEP 1: SEE WHERE IT GOES
7. **Step one is boring, and it is the most powerful: find out where your money goes.**
8. **For one week, write down every naira you spend. Every transfer, every airtime, every snack.**
9. **Most people are shocked. Small amounts add up fast.**
10. **Data bundles. Airtime. Takeaway food. Betting. Transfer fees. "Small small" spending.**
11. **They feel tiny one by one, but together they can swallow a fifth or more of a small salary.**
12. **You cannot fix what you cannot see.**

## STEP 2: PAY YOURSELF FIRST
13. **Step two: pay yourself first.**
14. **The moment your salary lands, move a fixed amount away, before you spend anything.**
15. **Start with ten percent. For fifty thousand naira, that is five thousand.**
16. **It will feel small. It is not.**
17. **Five thousand naira a month is sixty thousand in a year, before any growth.**
18. **In three years, it is one hundred and eighty thousand.**
19. **Set up an automatic transfer on salary day, so you never have to decide.**
20. **Treat it like rent. It is rent you pay to your future.**

## STEP 3: PLUG THE LEAKS
21. **Step three: plug one leak.**
22. **Look at your week of notes. Pick the leak that hurts the most and gives you the least.**
23. **Maybe it is betting. Maybe it is daily takeaway. Maybe it is too many data bundles.**
24. **Cut it by half. Not to zero, just half.**
25. **Small cuts you can keep beat big cuts you drop in a week.**
26. **Put what you save straight into your own account, so it does not drift back into spending.**

## STEP 4: GROW THE INCOME
27. **Step four: saving is only half of the story. The other half is earning more.**
28. **There is a limit to how much you can cut. There is no limit to how much you can grow your income.**
29. **Learn one skill people pay for. Design, writing, video editing, sales, bookkeeping, repairs.**
30. **Practise it a little every day, and offer it to one person this month.**
31. **Even an extra ten thousand naira a month changes the whole picture.**
32. **Your salary is a starting point, not a ceiling.**

## STEP 5: LET MONEY WORK
33. **Step five: put your savings somewhere they are safe, and can grow.**
34. **First, build an emergency fund: enough for one month of basic costs, then slowly toward three.**
35. **Keep it somewhere you can reach, but not so easy that you spend it.**
36. **Once it is built, learn about regulated options, like savings accounts, fixed deposits, treasury bills and mutual funds.**
37. **Each one has different risks and returns. Learn how they work before you put money in.**
38. **And remember the golden rule: if someone promises fast, guaranteed, huge returns, walk away.**
39. **Check that any company is registered and licensed, and never invest money you need for rent or food.**

## ENDING
40. **Wealth does not start with a big salary. It starts with a small system you keep.**
41. **See where it goes. Pay yourself first. Plug one leak. Grow your income. Let your savings work, safely.**
42. **Do step two today. Open your banking app, and set up that first small automatic transfer.**
43. **Five thousand naira. Ten percent. Every month.**
44. **A year from now, you will be glad you started today.**

**END CARD:** *Which step will you start today? Tell us in the comments.*
