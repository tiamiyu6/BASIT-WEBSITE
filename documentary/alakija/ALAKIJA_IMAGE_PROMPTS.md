# ALAKIJA: picture prompts (24)

Project: `Alakija`

```
Flat 2D cartoon illustration with bold dark outlines, bright warm colours (sunny yellow, orange, green, sky blue), simple friendly shapes, clean flat shading, like a kids' 'explained like you're 5' cartoon. NOT a photograph, NOT 3D, NOT realistic. Wide 16:9, no text, no letters, no numbers. The main character is a cartoon Nigerian woman (a young secretary who grows into a businesswoman): brown skin, a colourful gele head-tie, round glasses and a bright ankara dress; friendly face, the same in every picture. A cartoon character, not a portrait of a real person.
```

**Picture 01**: A young Nigerian woman in a smart blouse typing at a typewriter in a busy Lagos office, stacks of letters, a ringing phone, a sunny window.

**Picture 02**: The same young woman taking notes beside a boss at a big desk, a thought bubble above her showing a small shining dream of a factory and a ship.

**Picture 03**: The woman, now older and confident in a bright gele, standing in front of a glowing golden map of Africa with oil rigs and a crown above Nigeria.

**Picture 04**: A young woman on an airplane window seat looking out at London landmarks, a suitcase and a notebook on her lap.

**Picture 05**: A busy bank office in Lagos: the woman, in a neat dress, working at a desk; coworkers and files around her; she watches the managers carefully.

**Picture 06**: A split picture: on the left a comfortable desk with a safe steady chair, on the right an open door with golden light; the woman looks at the door.

**Picture 07**: The woman in a fashion school studio, mannequins and rolls of colourful ankara fabric around her, sketching a dress.

**Picture 08**: A small tailoring shop with a bright sign (no readable words), the woman measuring a smiling customer, sewing machines in a row.

**Picture 09**: A glamorous party scene: elegant Nigerian women in beautiful dresses made by the woman, who watches proudly from the side.

**Picture 10**: A chain of speech bubbles passing from one smiling customer to another and up to a fancy lady in a big house, showing word-of-mouth.

**Picture 11**: A glowing door in a wall opening to bright light, the woman standing in front of it with an application form in her hand.

**Picture 12**: A government office with officials around a table giving out rolled-up maps marked with coloured blocks; the woman holds a map.

**Picture 13**: A crowd of surprised people with question marks over their heads looking at the woman holding her map; she smiles calmly.

**Picture 14**: The woman shaking hands with engineers in hard hats beside an oil rig model on a table, drawings and helmets around.

**Picture 15**: A massive offshore oil platform in a bright blue sea with a tanker ship beside it, workers in helmets waving.

**Picture 16**: A calendar with many pages flying away, a growing pile of coins, a worried engineer and a sun rising slowly over the platform.

**Picture 17**: A snail on a road sign with a platform in the background and the woman patiently waiting with a flask of tea.

**Picture 18**: Oil flowing through pipes into big tanks, workers cheering, the woman smiling and clapping, a golden glow.

**Picture 19**: A golden list on a wall with a few women's silhouettes, and one star above the silhouette of the woman in a gele (no readable text).

**Picture 20**: A scale of fairness with admiration on one side (clapping crowd) and questions on the other (question marks); the woman beside it.

**Picture 21**: A group of widows and children smiling at a foundation building with the woman handing out school bags and food parcels.

**Picture 22**: A big heart shaped from many hands coming together, the woman in the middle with a warm smile.

**Picture 23**: A classroom chalkboard with five simple icons: a book, a sewing machine, a handshake, an hourglass and a heart; the woman teaches the first.

**Picture 24**: The businesswoman faces the viewer with a thumbs up; five small icons float in a circle behind her; a bell icon glows at the side.
