# WHAT IF YOU WOKE UP AS AFRICA'S BIGGEST SUPERSTAR?: script (draft 1)

Format: realistic cinematic stills, second-person story, AI narration. About 3 minutes.

**Fictional character. Never name a real artiste, and never put a real person's likeness or private story in it.**

---

## COLD OPEN
1. **You wake up. The ceiling is higher than you remember.**
2. **The bed is bigger. The sheets are softer. And your phone says: good morning, superstar.**
3. **You are thirty-five. No kids, no debt, and more money than you could spend in ten lifetimes.**
4. **Welcome to the life of the biggest artiste in Africa.**

## CHAPTER 1: THE MORNING
5. **Your manager walks in with your coffee. "Boss, the jet is ready."**
6. **Outside, three cars wait in the driveway. Each one costs more than a whole street in your old neighbourhood.**
7. **You don't even have to choose. You have a guy for that. He keeps them clean, fuelled and ready.**
8. **And somewhere nearby, your right hand man is already on the phone, handling everything you forget.**
9. **You feel like a king. For about eleven minutes.**

## CHAPTER 2: THE PHONE
10. **Then, ding. A notification.**
11. **You are trending. Not for a hit song. For something you said years ago.**
12. **A clip. A promise. A story that was never finished.**
13. **Somebody dug it up, and now the whole internet is awake.**
14. **A woman posts online, calmly. And everyone takes her side.**
15. **Your team panics. Three phones ring at once.**
16. **Everyone has an opinion about what you should say.**

## CHAPTER 3: THE CHOICE
17. **You could delete everything. You could hire a lawyer. You could just ignore it.**
18. **But you remember something your mother once said: when you make a promise, even a small one, someone is waiting.**
19. **So you type a reply. Short. Honest. A little funny.**
20. **You admit it. You apologise. And then you make a joke about yourself.**
21. **Within an hour, the internet changes its mind.**

## CHAPTER 4: THE LESSON
22. **That evening, you stand backstage. Forty thousand people are screaming your name.**
23. **And you realise something strange.**
24. **All the cars, all the money, all the people, and none of it protected you from one small broken promise.**
25. **Fame makes everything louder. Including your mistakes.**
26. **The only thing that fixed it is the oldest tool there is: telling the truth, quickly.**
27. **Then the lights go up, and you walk on stage.**

## ENDING
28. **Now here is the real question.**
29. **If you woke up as the biggest star in Africa tomorrow, what is the first thing you would do?**
30. **Tell us in the comments. And remember: the bigger you are, the more your small promises matter.**

**END CARD:** *What would you do first? Tell us in the comments.*
