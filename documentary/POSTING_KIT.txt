TITLE:
JAGABAN: How Bola Tinubu Rose to Power | Full Documentary

DESCRIPTION:
He has been called the godfather of Lagos, the kingmaker, the Jagaban.
This documentary traces how Bola Ahmed Tinubu rose to power: from his mother's market-women network in Lagos and an accounting career in Chicago, through the fight against General Abacha and years in exile, to eight years as Governor of Lagos, the making of the APC, and the 2023 presidency.

It also covers the questions that have followed him: the 1993 U.S. forfeiture case, the certificate dispute, the bullion vans and #EndSARS. Survivors of the Lekki toll gate shooting tell their own stories. Finally, it sets his 2012 opposition to fuel subsidy removal against "Fuel subsidy is gone."

Allegations are reported as allegations. Tinubu has denied wrongdoing in each case, and none of the controversies described has led to a criminal conviction.
