# ENGINE VIDEO: grid prompts (16 grids of 4 scenes + 1 thumbnail)

Each grid is ONE wide picture split into 2 by 2 panels. I cut each grid into 4 pictures, and make 3 to 4 shots from each.

```
Flat 2D cartoon illustration with bold dark outlines, bright warm colours (sunny yellow, orange, green, sky blue), simple friendly shapes, clean flat shading, like a kids' explainer cartoon. NOT a photograph, NOT 3D, NOT realistic. No text, no letters, no numbers. Recurring cast: Tunde is a cartoon Nigerian young man with round glasses and a mustard-yellow hoodie. Auntie Ngozi is a cartoon Nigerian woman in a bright green gele head-tie, always shocked or amused. Historical people are drawn as friendly cartoons in the clothes of their time. These are cartoon characters, not portraits of real people.
```

**Grid 01** (scenes 1 to 4):
- top-left: Dawn on a Lagos street: a neighbour's generator roars with a puff of smoke while Tunde yawns at his window.
- top-right: Tunde plugs his phone into a glowing socket, then kicks the starter of a small motorbike that coughs and hums.
- bottom-left: A bus, a plane and a ship side by side in one picture, each with a small glowing flame inside; Tunde looks puzzled.
- bottom-right: A cutaway of a tiny thimble-sized cylinder with tiny controlled explosions flashing inside.

**Grid 02** (scenes 5 to 8):
- top-left: Tunde pointing at a shiny engine with a big question mark above his head, Auntie Ngozi curious beside him.
- top-right: A campfire with rising hot-air arrows while Tunde warms his hands.
- bottom-left: A cutaway cylinder with glowing hot gas pushing a piston, which turns a wheel.
- bottom-right: A road with icons along it: a muscle arm, a windmill, a waterwheel, a Greek toy and a flooded mine; Tunde steps onto it.

**Grid 03** (scenes 9 to 12):
- top-left: Ancient farmers with oxen, horses pulling carts and workers carrying heavy loads.
- top-right: A tired ox lying next to an empty food basket.
- bottom-left: A sailing ship and a windmill, both with big wind arrows.
- bottom-right: A watermill beside a river, and next to it a dry riverbed with a sad miller.

**Grid 04** (scenes 13 to 16):
- top-left: Hero of Alexandria, a bearded ancient Greek inventor in a robe, beside a pot of boiling water with a hollow metal ball with two bent pipes on top.
- top-right: The ball spinning with steam jets while Hero and Tunde look amazed.
- bottom-left: The spinning ball on a shelf like a toy, people walking past without interest, a lightbulb floating above Hero.
- bottom-right: A 1600s scientist holding a glass tube of mercury, with air-pressure arrows pressing down.

**Grid 05** (scenes 17 to 20):
- top-left: Two huge copper half-spheres joined together, with teams of horses pulling opposite ways and failing.
- top-right: Air-pressure arrows pushing on one side of a sealed bowl, Tunde pointing at them.
- bottom-left: Denis Papin sketching a piston in a cylinder: steam rising on one side, cold on the other, arrows showing the push.
- bottom-right: An unfinished sketch on a desk and a puzzled inventor scratching his head.

**Grid 06** (scenes 21 to 24):
- top-left: Thomas Savery with a strange steam pump beside a flooded mine.
- top-right: A friendly miner drawn next to the pump, lifting a bucket of water, with a little sign-picture of a helping hand.
- bottom-left: The pump leaking steam, miners stepping back nervously.
- bottom-right: An old British town with few trees and a big pile of black coal.

**Grid 07** (scenes 25 to 28):
- top-left: A flooded coal mine tunnel with water rising around miners' boots.
- top-right: Horses walking in a circle to drive a slow pump, a tired groom beside them.
- bottom-left: Thomas Newcomen, a blacksmith in a leather apron, thinking at an anvil with sketches.
- bottom-right: A stone castle on a hill with a tall engine house beside a mine, people watching.

**Grid 08** (scenes 29 to 32):
- top-left: A cutaway of a cylinder with a piston and steam inside.
- top-right: Cold water spraying into the cylinder, steam collapsing, and big arrows from the air pushing the piston down.
- bottom-left: A big wooden beam rocking like a seesaw above an engine house, a pump below in the mine shaft.
- bottom-right: Workers shovelling huge piles of coal into a furnace while a mine owner smiles.

**Grid 09** (scenes 33 to 36):
- top-left: A map of Britain and Europe dotted with little engine-house icons, Tunde pointing.
- top-right: Young James Watt in a Glasgow workshop with a small model engine and tools.
- bottom-left: A cutaway: the cylinder alternately red-hot and ice-cold beside a thermometer, a shrinking coal pile, Watt scratching his head.
- bottom-right: Watt walking through a park on a Sunday with a lightbulb above his head.

**Grid 10** (scenes 37 to 40):
- top-left: A hot cylinder joined by a pipe to a separate cold chamber marked with a snowflake icon.
- top-right: A patent paper with a seal, and Watt looking at his empty pockets.
- bottom-left: Watt shaking hands with a smiling businessman in a top hat in a workshop.
- bottom-right: Two engines side by side: one with a huge coal pile, the other with a quarter-size pile.

**Grid 11** (scenes 41 to 44):
- top-left: Watt's engine turning a big wheel, with belts and gears.
- top-right: A factory full of machines driven by belts from one steam engine, chimneys smoking.
- bottom-left: A big heavy engine locked inside a stone building by chains, Watt waving beside it.
- bottom-right: Richard Trevithick, a tall Cornish engineer, beside a small strong high-pressure engine on a wooden frame.

**Grid 12** (scenes 45 to 48):
- top-left: A clumsy three-wheeled steam carriage in an old French street bumping into a wall.
- top-right: A little steam locomotive pulling wagons of iron along a track in a Welsh valley.
- bottom-left: Steam locomotives and steamships crossing a world map.
- bottom-right: A boiler bursting with a puff of steam while engineers with notebooks take notes.

**Grid 13** (scenes 49 to 52):
- top-left: Sadi Carnot, a young Frenchman at a desk with a question mark above his head, a steam engine in the window.
- top-right: A waterfall with a little wheel at the bottom: a flame icon at the top, a snowflake at the bottom, arrows between.
- bottom-left: Heat arrows escaping from an engine like a leaking cup, and a glowing science book.
- bottom-right: An engine with a separate boiler outside and a big steam cloud, next to a smaller engine with a flame inside its cylinder.

**Grid 14** (scenes 53 to 56):
- top-left: Étienne Lenoir with a small gas engine in a Paris workshop, gas lamps in the window.
- top-right: Nicolaus Otto beside a cutaway cylinder showing four small panels: suck in, squeeze, burn, push out, using icons only.
- bottom-left: Rudolf Diesel beside a cutaway cylinder where squeezed air glows red-hot with no spark.
- bottom-right: A wooden-framed first petrol motorbike with Daimler and Maybach standing proudly beside it.

**Grid 15** (scenes 57 to 60):
- top-left: Karl Benz in a light three-wheeled motor carriage in a German street, startled horses nearby.
- top-right: Bertha Benz driving the carriage with two boys along a country road towards a distant town.
- bottom-left: A collage of a car, a bus, a plane, a ship and a generator behind a Lagos house.
- bottom-right: A long chain of different inventors, each handing a part to the next.

**Grid 16** (scenes 61 to 64):
- top-left: Tunde with his ear against a humming generator at dawn, tiny spark explosions inside.
- top-right: A waterfall of heat from a hot orb to a cold orb, with a small bucket catching some.
- bottom-left: Tunde and a shocked Auntie Ngozi pointing at a big question mark.
- bottom-right: A glowing lightbulb with a lightning bolt, Tunde waving and a bell icon at the side.
